Autism groups in Brisbane

Looking for autistic and neurodivergent people to spend time with? BASS is a social community built for exactly that.

What is BASS?

Brisbane Autism Social Society (BASS) is a community for adults who are autistic, have AuDHD or are otherwise neurodivergent. We help people meet each other, make friends and join in social activities without pressure.

BASS is based in Brisbane and focused on South East Queensland.

What to expect

Our focus is friendship and social connection at a relaxed pace.

Common questions

Who is BASS for?

BASS is for autistic adults, adults with AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

Is BASS a therapy or clinical service?

No. BASS is a social community focused on friendship and connection. It does not provide therapy or medical services.

Where is BASS based?

BASS is based in Brisbane and focused on South East Queensland. Event locations are listed on the events page.
