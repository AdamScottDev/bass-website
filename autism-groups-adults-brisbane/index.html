Autism groups for adults in Brisbane

Making friends as an adult can be hard, especially when busy or unpredictable social settings don't suit you. BASS offers a calmer way to meet people.

A place to meet people

BASS is a social group and friendship community for autistic and neurodivergent adults. You can come along, meet people and take part at your own pace.

Social, not clinical

BASS is a community, not a clinical service. We don't offer therapy or medical advice. We are here for friendship and connection.

Who can join

BASS is for adults who are autistic, have AuDHD or are otherwise neurodivergent, in Brisbane and South East Queensland.
