About BASS

Brisbane Autism Social Society is a social community for autistic, AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults.

What BASS stands for

BASS stands for Brisbane Autism Social Society.

What the community is

BASS is based in Brisbane and focused on South East Queensland. We are a community of adults who want to meet people and make friends.

Who it is for

  • Autistic adults
  • Adults with AuDHD
  • Other neurodivergent adults
  • People living in Brisbane and South East Queensland

Our approach to inclusion and social connection

BASS exists to help adults meet people, make friends and take part in social activities in a low-pressure environment. You can join in at your own pace.

What BASS is not

BASS is not a clinical service. We do not provide therapy, diagnosis or medical advice. For medical support, contact your GP or local health service. In an emergency, call 000.
