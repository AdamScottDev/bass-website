About BASS

Brisbane Autism Social Society is a social community for autistic, AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults.

Who we are

BASS is based in Brisbane and focused on South East Queensland. We are a community of adults who want to meet people and make friends.

Why BASS exists

To help adults meet people, make friends and take part in social activities in a low-pressure environment.

What BASS is not

BASS is not a clinical service. We do not provide therapy, diagnosis or medical advice.
