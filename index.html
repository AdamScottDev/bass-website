A friendly social community for autistic and neurodivergent adults in Brisbane

Brisbane Autism Social Space (BASS) helps adults meet people, make friends and take part in social activities, in a relaxed, low-pressure setting.

What is BASS?

A social community for adults in Brisbane. Our aim is simple: meet people, make friends and feel at ease while you do it.

Who is it for?

Autistic adults, adults with AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

How do I join?

Join our Discord. That is where the community is.

What you can expect

  • A community for adults, focused on friendship.
  • A relaxed, low-pressure setting. You can take part at your own pace.
  • No therapy or medical services. BASS is a social community, not a clinical service.
