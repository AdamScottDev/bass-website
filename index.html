What BASS is

For adults

BASS is a social community for adults.

Autistic and neurodivergent

Autistic adults, adults with AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults are at the heart of the community.

Based in Brisbane

BASS is based in Brisbane and focused on South East Queensland.

Friendship first

The aim is simple: meet people, make friends and feel at ease while you do it.

A community, not a clinical service

BASS does not provide therapy or medical services. It is a place for friendship, social connection and community.
