Join our community

Make a free account, set up your profile and chat with other autistic, ADHD and neurodivergent adults in South East Queensland.

Create your free account

At least 8 characters.

We have sent you an email. Select the link in it to verify your address, then log in.

How it works

  1. Create a free account. You only need an email address.
  2. Verify your email. We send you a link to confirm it is you.
  3. Make your profile. Choose a display name and add a short bio if you want to.
  4. Join or open a Pod. A Pod is a chat room. Open your own or join one made by another member.

Take your time

BASS is low-pressure. You can read first, join in when you are ready and leave a Pod whenever you like.

Brisbane Autism Social Society (BASS)
A social chat community for autistic, ADHD and neurodivergent adults in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

BASS is a community, not a clinical or therapy service. In an emergency, call 000.
