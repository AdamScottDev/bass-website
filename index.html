What is BASS?

BASS (Brisbane Autism Social Space) is an autism and neurodivergent social group for adults in Brisbane. We run relaxed social events and an online community where you can chat, share your interests and gradually get to know people.

Making friends isn't always easy. Some people need more time to feel comfortable around others. Some find traditional social groups overwhelming or hard to navigate. Others haven't found the right people yet.

BASS is designed to make socialising easier. Our goal is simple: to help neurodivergent adults in Brisbane find their people.

Who is BASS for?

  • Autistic adults
  • Adults with ADHD or AuDHD
  • Other neurodivergent adults
  • People in Brisbane and South East Queensland

BASS is a community for adults. It is not a clinic, a therapy service or an NDIS provider.

What makes BASS different?

  • Made for adults. This is a community for adults, not a youth or family group.
  • No social performance. Nobody expects you to be good at socialising.
  • Online and in person. Start by chatting online. Events are optional.
  • A community, not a service. We don't offer therapy or medical advice. In an emergency, call 000.

How joining works

  1. Select Join the BASS Discord.
  2. Log in to Discord, or create a free account.
  3. Follow Discord's prompts to join the community.
  4. Look around, and say hello when you feel ready.

Questions about BASS

Is BASS only for autistic people?

No. BASS is for autistic, ADHD, AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults in Brisbane.

Is BASS for adults?

Yes. BASS is a community for adults.

Where is BASS located?

BASS is based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. Our social events are in Brisbane and our online community is on Discord.

Do I need to be good at socialising?

No. You do not need to be outgoing or good at small talk. You can take things at your own pace.

Do I have to attend in-person events?

No. You can join the Discord and chat online. Events are there if and when you want to come along.

How do I join BASS?

Select Join the BASS Discord and follow the prompts. You need a Discord account, which is free to create.
