Meet our community

BASS is a friendship community for autistic and neurodivergent adults in Brisbane. Here is a look at who is in it, and how you can start meeting people.

Take your time

BASS is a low-pressure community. You can take part at your own pace.

Introductions from our members

These posts are shared from our Discord with each member's permission. We remove names, usernames, photos and links.

How to start

  1. Join our Discord. This is where the BASS community is.
  2. Get comfortable. Look around and join in when you feel ready.
  3. Come to an event if you want to. Events are listed on the Events page.

Who you will meet

Autistic adults, adults with AuDHD and other neurodivergent adults in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

Brisbane Autism Social Society (BASS)
A social community for autistic and neurodivergent adults in Brisbane and South East Queensland.

BASS is a community, not a clinical or therapy service. In an emergency, call 000.
